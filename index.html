A little about us

Fragrance is

CELVOR is an independent fragrance house in the making, built around a simple idea: the best scent feels like you, a little more.

We’re exploring thoughtful ingredients, unexpected contrasts, and the small rituals that make a fragrance your own. This is our first study—a starting point, shaped one experiment at a time.
